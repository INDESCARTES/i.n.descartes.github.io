There's a quiet part of taking the path to authorship that many people whisper about in forums, but you have to specifically demand to know. Following your dreams is so expensive.

It's not even funny how many posts you see of successful authors finally deciding to let the public know how much they spent on their art, films, and novels. And it's always a number that makes me wince and shake my head.

It's not to say that I think spending that kind of money is a bad thing. If I'm going to work a job I don't particularly like, it's better to have that money go toward something I enjoy. Character art, dev and line editors, audio equipment, a stylish background.

At this point, I'm just listing things I really want. LOL.

The art of making a novel you can look at and be proud of relies on you being willing to sacrifice a lot for the fun of writing. Even things you don't think about — the novels that influence your writing style, the movies that inspire that one scene that looked so cinematic in your mind, the food you eat when you're on a writing binge. All that money masterfully blends together and becomes your art.

Isn't that so wonderful?
