Daniel Umbridge Montogomey was by all measures accounted for in the ways that matter, a well established man.

He was exhorbitantly wealthy, handsome to a reasonable extent, as well as blessed with a sense of charm that left many people feelig particularly inscenced to help him, whether he needed it or not.

Daniel used to think of it as something akin to a superpower. A quick smile blessed him with teacher favorability, and the love of his classmates. Innate talent with his hands and body made him a comrade to those that admired feats of physical strength and team sport.

And wasn't it something that due to all of his innate charm and beauty, his parents held off on his transformation into a creature of dangerous nature and chemically altered features. There hasn't been a pressing urgency to imbue him with charmer. It had been a great pride to Daniel.

Unlike his plucky sister, who had been thrown into a part of the house to scream and cry her way through the transformation at eight years old. Daniel had watched over her carefully, as a good brother should. He brought her towels to clean up her vomit and sweat. He brought her vaccum sealed bags of tender flesh locally sourced, of course.

He wrapped her gently in blankets as she shivered and hid herself from mirrors as the veins in her arteries bulged and jerked horrendously in sporadic rhythms. He listened to her babbling and whimpers with a patient amusement of a father listening to his child writhing with a fever.

After it passed, she looked over and saw Daniel sitting comfortably on her lounge chair in the corner of the room. On shaky weak limbs she sat up and casted him a look of disgust.

"What are you doing here?" Daniel gave her a patient smile before bending over and grabbing a wooden tray. He stood up smoothly and placed the try in her lap. She scrunched up her nose and looked up to Daniel as he continued his act as a loving provider.

"Mother and father wanted to make sure that you eat. The transformation is very hard on the body, I've come to understand." Portia sneered at me. The chubbiness that clung to her cheeks so lovingly had melted off in thick globs. Her eyes were sharp and cold as Daniel continued to smile down at her, emulating a angelic statue in a chapel. It was a particular skill that he possessed. In fact.

"Get that away from me," She hissed shoving the tray away. It wobbled violently, but Daniel caught it before the porcelain plate shattered. Daniel took a step back, lazily holding his hands up in mock surrender.

"I'm just here to deliver your food. Nothing more."

"Well you've done it." She snapped, glaring at Daniel with vitriol. "So get out." Daniel retreated from his sister's room. Letting her recouperate by herself.

If Daniel was left with peace and quiet for a month due to her never leaving her room, he wasn't one to worry about it. This story wasn't even about her, no this was a story about Daniel Umbridge Montgomery.

Yes, Daniel Umbridge Montgomery was an amalgamation of luck and charm. However there was one thing that he desired that he lacked so thuroughly that it was almost comical. Almost.

He lacked a certain amount of intelligence.

It was a curse the Daniel Montgomery was essentially vexed with. Starting from third grade and all the way through high school. No matter how hard he studied and poured into the art of academia, he couldn't grasp it in a meaningful or consistent way.

His sister, the vain disgusting creature that his father loved, never had that issue. During her little stint of isolation, she apparently was able to keep up with her studies through intuition and pure luck. His father would say skill, but Daniel wasn't one to go that far.

It was one of the things that elluded him. When he turned in papers, teachers would kindly pull him aside after class and give him second, third, fourth chances. Numbers melted and Chernenko together beneath his gaze, meanings and messages in passages simply didn't mean anything to him. It was as if he was a bowl already overfilled with the sweet wines of all his other gifts, that the one he tried to embue within himself just couldn't find space.

You could say that in his own way, Daniel was quite naieve to believe that this was how intelligence worked. That's seemed to be how it worked for his sibling. Portia bounced back and soaked in the attention that was given to her from her "bout of illness," with a new voracity for attention.

She gained it easily, just like Daniel had. Loud, blunt, and aggressive. The only difference was that she had the intelligence to back up her words. There was no need for charm and persuasion if you had the skill and information readily available at the forefront of your mind, Daniel begrudgingly supposes.

But that hadn't mattered, all that much, because Daniel had finally found a means of escape. A letter of reccomendation from his father's desk to attend Boston University. He held the tablet that had the open email in his hand. This was the moment that he had been waiting for.

His father had scoffed when he was made it known that he wanted to go to Boston for University. "I'm sorry," he had chuckled leaned over his mahogany desk heavily. He rubbed his face before giving Daniel a smile that made him naseous. "I hadn't meant to laugh, it's just…Do you think that you'll be able to keep up with the…Academic rigor of our Alma Mater?" At the pause, his father tilted his head.

"It's not a jest, I;m being completely serious." Daniel swallowed thickly, his Adam's apple bobbing slightly.

"I can handle it father." His father groaned, Daniel winced internally. He briefly thought that perhaps he intruded at a bad time. He distantly remembered his mother threatening to have a salcious affair with his father's mistress in his summer house and sent a tape for him to watch if he kept parading her around.

Wouldn't now be a _fantastic_ time for that to have dropped when he needed the environment to favor him.

"See, all I can remember is the amount of messages my personal assistant sent me about how you're soooo…Great at everything else, but goodness you couldn't understand just about anything your teachers, and personally tutors tried to get past that thick head of yours." His father stepped towards his leather upholsterered office chair.

"You can see why I'm worried, yes?" Daniel's lips thinned, his back felt tense for the ramrod posture.

"Yes father, I understand." His father studied his face impassively before snapping his fingers and leaning back into his chair.

"How about we make a trade? You know how those work, don't you Daniel?" Daniel raised an eyebrow, a trade? He expected laughter, and perhaps some begging on his part. Daniel had been prepared to negotiate. But not a trade…

"What are the terms?" He asked cautiously. His father waved away the apprehension that was coming off of Daniel in waves.

"Don't look like that. You're making me feel like the bad guy," his father groused. Daniel took a step forward, he made sure to smooth out his expression into something more genile.

"Sorry, what are your terms father?" His father gave him a thin lipped smile.

"I want you to start takin Charmer. Daniel felt the pit of his stomach drop. The thought of the change and the consequences…

"Could I ask why? It's not as thought it will do much to improve my appearance." Had his father or others suspected that his looks were falling out of favor? Is that was his sister had been trying to get closer to him? To be inches away while he world was rocked by this downfall?

His father leaned back into his chair, and reached for his drawer. "The newest iterations have been found to improve cognitive function and I.Q. I think that it would be perfect for you if you were to pursue this endeavor. Don't you think?" Finally finding what he was looking for, he pulls out a small bottle of charmer , the color stark against the deep browns and greys that made up his father's study.

"A reccomendation decried my a rich alumni, all for the price of intelligence. Doesn't that sound marvelous?" His father had a smile on his face, but it fell flat when paired with the emptiness in his eyes. Daniel grimaced.

"Will you accept different forms of negotiation?" His father hummed and thought about it.

"What's your GPA currently?" Daniel paused, before clenching his jaw. his father most likely know what his GPA was. To make him say it was humiliating.

"It's 2.5, sir." His father snorted derisively.

"Right, is there a way that you could get a 4.0? If so, I'll let you go. You'd have proven yourself at that point."

There was no way that could happen. For all Daniel's charm, there was no way that his teachers would simply allow for him to replace the many C's and B'd shameful D's with A's all so that he could escape this hellscape of a home. Daniel took a few steps forward and shakily took the glass bottle.

"I don't what Portia to look after me during the transformation." His father shrugged.

"Don't, she's going to boarding school overseas anyway. She had another gluttonous episode." His father rolled his eyes. Daniel felt a bit soothed at his sisters step down from grace. "If there's one thing that his family doesn't need, it's a messy eater. You will be giving an attendant that had looked over others that have overseen the change. Though I suspect that it shouldn't be as tremoltuous as your sister's transformation. I hope that you;ll be a man about this whole situation."

Daniel nodded his afformation before tentatively taking the glasss bottle from his father's grasp. His father watched him carefully. "Go on, take the drink." Daniel took a breath before unsealing the top and downing the contents quickly. The sweetness, aced with a matallic undertone felt unfamiliar on his tastebuds. He wrinkled his nose and licked his lips.

His father clapped his hands together, before pulling out his phone and dialing a number, After a slight pause, the person on the other end picked up at the fourth ring.

"Lancer, it's great to talk to you again, say really fast, your still working for BU admissions, right? Great, I have a proposition for you if you have time to hear it." Glancing over at Daniel who was beginning to sway as the drink began to take effect, he waved him out of his study, which Daniel quickly obliged.

Stumbling out, and navigating his way clumsily towards his bedroom. His shut the door soundly behind him before finally collapsing onto the carpet next to his bed. He curled up within himself as the effects started to send shooting pains through his limbs. Daniels vision swam as he began to drip with sweat.

This would all be worth it, he thought to himself deliriously.

This will all be worth it to finally become perfect.
